How does Gestura work with remote classes?

Open your teaching workspace, allow camera access for hand gestures, and use the Tools menu to choose a whiteboard or PDF. Share this browser tab or window through your existing meeting app. Gestura does not host participant calls, record sessions, or save notes after closing the page.

How do I draw and control tools with one finger?

Extend only your index finger and fold your other fingers. Hold over a button until the ring completes to select it. Move over a board to draw, then fold your index finger to lift the brush. Use the brush size buttons for finer lines or wider marks.
